Built For Downtown Moline Since 2018

Founded by local owner Hong Nguyen and backed by a passionate team of Quad Cities beer and bourbon fans, Central Store started with a simple mission: give downtown Moline a spotless, friendly neighborhood corner store — then surprise every visitor with one of the best-curated craft beer and bourbon walls in the Midwest.

Everything Under One Roof

More Than A Bottle Shop

Whether you're hunting rare Chicago IPAs, picking up a gift bottle of top-shelf scotch, or grabbing morning Harvest Ridge coffee and snacks on 5th Ave, we keep our aisles bright, clean, and well-stocked from 6 AM until late night.

01 / CRAFT BEER

304+ Cold Cans & Singles

Mix-and-match single cans, hyped Wednesday/Thursday Midwest drops, imperial stouts, fruited sours, and crisp local lagers kept ice-cold in our illuminated walk-in cooler wall.

02 / BOURBON & SPIRITS

Store Picks & Rare Bottles

Allocated Kentucky bourbons, hand-picked rickhouse single barrels, high-end scotches, gift sets, and cognac ready-to-serve cocktails curated for everyday sippers and collectors.

03 / ZERO-PROOF & HEMP (21+)

Massive NA & THC Wall

One of the Quad Cities' largest lineups of non-alcoholic beers, hop waters, craft sodas, and 21+ hemp-derived THC sodas from Oliphant, Looner, Eclectric & more.

04 / DOWNTOWN MARKET

Coffee, Snacks & Essentials

Fresh Harvest Ridge coffee station, cold grab-and-go food & snacks, chips, soda, everyday grocery staples, and fast in-store pickup right in the heart of Moline Centre.

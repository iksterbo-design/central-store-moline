# Central Store & Central Craft — Downtown Moline, IL

Official flagship website for **Central Store** (`1810 5th Ave, Suite D`) and companion taproom **Central Craft** (`1810 5th Ave, Suite F`) in downtown Moline, Illinois.

## Features
- **Interactive "Find Your Pour" Sommelier** — flavor mood matcher across Hazy IPAs, Barrel-Aged Stouts, Fruited Sours, Crisp Lagers, Allocated Bourbons, and Zero-Proof / 21+ Hemp drinks
- **Live Craft Beer, Cooler, Bourbon & Zero-Proof Board** — 4 tabs with live search, style filters, sorting, ABV strength meters, and a persistent **My Visit List** drawer
- **Central Craft & Barrel Picks Showcase** — Suite F taproom highlights, rickhouse single-barrel program, and QC-exclusive ASW × John Deere 8-Year Fiddler whiskeys
- **Locally Owned Downtown Story** — celebrating owner Hong Nguyen, 2018 founding in Moline Centre, and local community involvement
- **Videos & Facebook Reels Showcase** — real reels from `@centralstoremoline` with modal player + direct Facebook links
- **Filterable Store Photo Gallery** — 10 real store photos with fullscreen keyboard lightbox
- **Special Bottle Request & Drop Desk** — quick availability/keg inquiry builder directly to `centralstorellc@gmail.com` or `(309) 524-4054`
- **Live Moline Central Time Status & Map** — real-time open/closed indicator, Suite D / Suite F toggle, and OpenStreetMap pin
